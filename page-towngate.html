Towngate is a master-planned community in western Pembroke Pines, just west of I-75 between Pines Boulevard and Sheridan Street, with about 1,830 single-family homes and 240 townhomes built from 1994 to 2000 around a chain of lakes. It's made up of 14 neighborhoods, most of them gated, and most of its homes have sold for about $550,000 to $800,000 over the last two years. Towngate at a Glance

Homes: about 1,830 single-family homes and 240 townhomes in county records, which matches the master association's count of 2,094 homes in 14 neighborhoods. The townhomes are in Park Crossing and Parkview.
Built: 1994 to 2000, most in 1995 and 1996.
Sizes: single-family homes are mostly 3 and 4 bedrooms, about 1,590 to 2,880 square feet (median about 2,080), and the largest are about 4,170. Townhomes are about 1,150 to 1,720 square feet.
Neighborhoods: Canary Bay, Cedar Way, Cherry Bay, Classic Landing, Classic Vista, Contempo Walk, Creekside, Governor's Run, Kingsview, Mahogany Way, Orchid Vista, Park Crossing, Parkview and Royal Bay. Most are gated: Canary Bay, Cedar Way, Cherry Bay, Classic Landing, Classic Vista, Contempo Walk, Creekside, Governor's Run, Kingsview, Orchid Vista and Royal Bay have gated entrances, while Mahogany Way and the townhome neighborhoods, Park Crossing and Parkview, are not gated.
Water: lakes run through the community, and about a third of the homes sold in the last two years were waterfront.
Amenities: landscaped common areas and wide sidewalks maintained by the master association, and many neighborhoods have their own pool and playground. Towngate Park, a City of Pembroke Pines park with a public pool and lighted tennis courts, is inside the community at 901 NW 155th Avenue, and so is Silver Palms Elementary. Bulk Comcast (Xfinity) internet and TV are part of the master association dues.
Shopping: two Publix supermarkets and other stores are on Pines Boulevard at the south edge of Towngate, and The Shops at Pembroke Gardens is about 3 miles away.
Streets: roughly Pines Boulevard north to Sheridan Street, from I-75 west to NW 160th Avenue (Dykes Road), ZIP code 33028.
Public schools assigned: for the 10 addresses I checked across Towngate with the Broward County Public Schools school locator in November 2026, every home was assigned to Silver Palms Elementary, Walter C. Young Middle and Charles W. Flanagan High. Boundaries can change, so check the exact address with Broward County Public Schools.

The HOA, Rental Rules and Rents

Towngate has two layers of association. Every home belongs to the Towngate at Pembroke Pines Master Association, managed by Castle Group, which maintains the common areas and landscaping and bills quarterly. The master dues include a bulk Comcast (Xfinity) contract for internet and TV; the association's website lists quarterly charges of $158 for the master common areas and $292 for Comcast. Most homes also belong to a neighborhood sub-association, which bills separately for things like its gate, pool and landscaping. In MLS listings sold over the last two years, the association fee shown was usually about $150 to $250 a month (median about $210), but the sub-association fee isn't always included, so ask for both amounts before you buy.

If you're buying to rent the home out, check the neighborhood's rules first, because they differ by sub-association. About 4 in 10 MLS listings over the last two years said the association must approve tenants, some neighborhoods don't allow leasing in the first one or two years of ownership, and Contempo Walk and Royal Bay publish their own tenant applications. Ask Castle Group and the sub-association for the current leasing rules before you buy a home to rent out.

Towngate has an active rental market. From November 2024 to November 2026, about 80 homes in Towngate were leased through the MLS. Single-family homes rented for a median of about $3,900 a month: about $3,700 for 3 bedrooms (most $3,150 to $4,400) and about $4,250 for 4 bedrooms (most $3,500 to $4,800). Three-bedroom townhomes rented for about $3,100 to $3,500. Rentals took a median of about 40 days to lease, and a little over half of the landlords allowed pets.

Property Taxes and Amendment 3

Taxes reset after a sale. At a price of about $670,000, plan on roughly $12,300 a year with homestead or about $13,000 without it (2026 Pembroke Pines rates, including the city fire assessment and drainage fee).

Florida voters passed Amendment 3 on November 3, 2026. For Florida residents with homestead, the exemption on non-school taxes goes from about $51,000 to $150,000 in 2027 and $250,000 in 2028, which at 2026 Pembroke Pines rates is about $1,200 less a year in 2027 and about $2,450 less a year in 2028. For a homesteaded Towngate home taxed at about $670,000, that would bring the bill from about $12,300 to roughly $11,100 in 2027 and $9,850 in 2028, if tax rates stay the same. If you already have homestead, you don't need to reapply. If you move to Florida after December 31, 2026, the non-school exemption starts at $50,000 and the larger amount begins in your fifth year of homestead. School taxes and the Save Our Homes cap don't change. See my Amendment 3 page, and ask me for an estimate on a specific home.

Selling in Towngate: Save Our Homes and Portability

About 85% of Towngate single-family homes have a homestead exemption, and on the 2026 county roll the typical homesteaded home here is assessed about $280,000 below its market value because of the Save Our Homes cap. When you sell, the buyer's taxes reset to market value, but you can usually transfer up to $500,000 of that Save Our Homes benefit to your next Florida homestead if you set it up within about three years. That's called portability, and it can make moving up or downsizing within Florida much more affordable than people expect. I'm happy to estimate your portability amount and what your home could sell for.

Flood Zone and Insurance

All 12 Towngate addresses I checked on FEMA's flood maps in November 2026, spread across the community, are in Zone X (shaded), outside the high-risk Special Flood Hazard Area, so lenders don't require flood insurance there. Some lakefront listings mention part of the lot in Zone AE, so for a home on the water, check the exact lot and ask for the elevation certificate. Flood insurance in Zone X is usually inexpensive and worth considering. Pembroke Pines is a Class 5 community in FEMA's Community Rating System, which can lower National Flood Insurance Program premiums by up to 25%. Most homes are from the mid 1990s, so the age of the roof, impact windows or shutters, and the wind mitigation report make the biggest difference in homeowners insurance.

Getting Around

Towngate sits just west of I-75, close to the exits at Pines Boulevard and Sheridan Street. From the middle of the community it's about 3 miles to The Shops at Pembroke Gardens, about 5 miles to Pembroke Lakes Mall and Memorial Hospital West, about 6 miles to Pembroke Pines City Hall and about 16 miles to Fort Lauderdale Hollywood International Airport. These are driving distances, and times vary with traffic.

Towngate FAQ

Where is Towngate in Pembroke Pines?

In western Pembroke Pines, just west of I-75, roughly between Pines Boulevard and Sheridan Street and east of NW 160th Avenue (Dykes Road), ZIP code 33028.

Is Towngate a gated community?

Most of it is. Eleven of its 14 neighborhoods have gated entrances: Canary Bay, Cedar Way, Cherry Bay, Classic Landing, Classic Vista, Contempo Walk, Creekside, Governor's Run, Kingsview, Orchid Vista and Royal Bay. Mahogany Way and the townhome neighborhoods, Park Crossing and Parkview, are not gated.

Are there townhomes in Towngate?

Yes. About 240 townhomes are in Park Crossing and Parkview. Over the last two years they sold for a median of about $521,000.

How much are the HOA fees in Towngate?

There is a quarterly master association fee, which includes bulk Comcast internet and TV, plus, for most homes, a separate neighborhood sub-association fee. In MLS listings sold over the last two years, the association fee shown was usually about $150 to $250 a month. Ask for both before you buy.

Can you rent out a home in Towngate?

Often yes, but the rules depend on the neighborhood: many require tenant approval, and some don't allow leasing in the first one or two years of ownership. Check with Castle Group and the sub-association first.

What does it cost to rent in Towngate?

Over the last two years, single-family homes leased for a median of about $3,900 a month, about $3,700 for 3 bedrooms and $4,250 for 4 bedrooms.

Does Towngate have a pool and tennis courts?

Many neighborhoods have their own pool. Towngate Park, a City of Pembroke Pines park inside the community at 901 NW 155th Avenue, has a public pool and lighted tennis courts.

What do homes in Towngate sell for?

From November 2024 to November 2026, recorded single-family sales had a median of about $670,000, with most between about $550,000 and $800,000.

Is Towngate in a flood zone?

The addresses I checked were all in Zone X, outside the high-risk flood zone, but some lakefront lots are partly in Zone AE. Check each address.

How does Amendment 3 affect Towngate property taxes?

For Florida residents with homestead, the non-school exemption rises to $150,000 in 2027 and $250,000 in 2028. Current homestead owners don't need to reapply. People who move to Florida after December 31, 2026 start at $50,000 and get the larger amount in their fifth year of homestead.
