Silver Lakes (written SilverLakes by its association) is one of the largest master-planned communities in Pembroke Pines, with about 2,340 single-family homes on the Pembroke Pines side, built from 1990 to 2001 around lakes on both sides of Pines Boulevard in the far west of the city. It's made up of dozens of smaller neighborhoods, some of them gated, and it's one of the most active areas in Pembroke Pines for homes around $700,000. Silver Lakes at a Glance

Homes: about 2,340 single-family homes on the Pembroke Pines side, plus about 335 townhomes. The larger SilverLakes community, about 5,186 homes in all, continues south of Pembroke Road into Miramar. This page covers the Pembroke Pines single-family homes.
Built: 1990 to 2001, most in the early 1990s.
Sizes: mostly 3 and 4 bedrooms. Most are about 1,600 to 2,950 square feet (median about 2,175), and the largest are close to 4,850.
Neighborhoods: the SilverLakes Community Association covers 39 neighborhoods on about 2,400 acres, such as Bermuda Village, Brittany Bay, Crystal Bay, Emerald Springs, Malibu Pointe, Sapphire Shores and Treasure Cay.
Water: lakes run through the community, and about a third of the homes sold in the last two years were waterfront.
Amenities: the master association has a community pool, a community park with a pavilion, a boaters' park and playgrounds, and a security patrol covers the community. Many neighborhoods also have their own pool, and some have entrance gates. Bulk cable TV and internet are included with the master association dues.
Shopping: Publix, Walmart, CVS and other stores are in the shopping centers along Pines Boulevard inside Silver Lakes, and Memorial Hospital Miramar is about 2 miles away.
Streets: roughly NW 21st Street south to Pembroke Road, between about 172nd and 184th Avenues, ZIP code 33029.
Public schools assigned: for the addresses I checked with the Broward County Public Schools school locator in November 2026, homes north of Pines Boulevard were assigned to Panther Run Elementary, Silver Trail Middle and West Broward High, and homes south of Pines Boulevard to Silver Lakes Elementary, Glades Middle and Everglades High. Boundaries can change, so check the exact address with Broward County Public Schools.

The HOA and Rental Rules

Silver Lakes has two layers of association. Every home belongs to the SilverLakes Community Association, the master association managed by Pines Property Management, which maintains the landscaped common areas, playgrounds, a community pool and private roads, and bills quarterly. Bulk cable TV and internet are included in the master dues. Most homes also belong to one of 39 neighborhood sub-associations, which maintain things like their own pool, landscaping and, in some neighborhoods, entrance gates or a manned gate, and bill separately. In MLS listings sold over the last two years, the association fee shown was usually about $190 to $310 a month (median about $230), but the sub-association fee isn't always included in that number, so ask for both the master and the sub-association amounts before you buy.

If you're buying to rent the home out, read the rules first. Under the rental amendment SilverLakes adopted in 2021 and the tenant approval process published by Pines Property Management, leases must be at least 12 months, owners must have owned the home for at least a year, rentals are capped at 10% of each neighborhood, and some neighborhoods, such as Emerald Springs, allow no rentals at all, while others are at or near their cap. Tenants have to be approved before moving in, including a background check and a minimum credit score. Ask Pines Property Management whether the neighborhood has a rental spot open before you buy a home to rent out.

Property Taxes and Amendment 3

Taxes reset after a sale. At a price of about $700,000, plan on roughly $12,800 a year with homestead or about $13,600 without it (2026 Pembroke Pines rates for most of Silver Lakes, including the city fire assessment and drainage fee).

Florida voters passed Amendment 3 on November 3, 2026. For Florida residents with homestead, the exemption on non-school taxes goes from about $51,000 to $150,000 in 2027 and $250,000 in 2028, which at 2026 Pembroke Pines rates is about $1,200 less a year in 2027 and about $2,450 less a year in 2028. For a homesteaded Silver Lakes home taxed at about $700,000, that would bring the bill from about $12,800 to roughly $11,600 in 2027 and $10,400 in 2028, if tax rates stay the same. If you already have homestead, you don't need to reapply. If you move to Florida after December 31, 2026, the non-school exemption starts at $50,000 and the larger amount begins in your fifth year of homestead. School taxes and the Save Our Homes cap don't change. See my Amendment 3 page, and ask me for an estimate on a specific home.

Selling in Silver Lakes: Save Our Homes and Portability

About 9 in 10 Silver Lakes homes have a homestead exemption, and on the 2026 county roll the typical homesteaded home here is assessed about $280,000 below its market value because of the Save Our Homes cap. When you sell, the buyer's taxes reset to market value, but you can usually transfer up to $500,000 of that Save Our Homes benefit to your next Florida homestead if you set it up within about three years. That's called portability, and it can make moving up or downsizing within Florida much more affordable than people expect. I'm happy to estimate your portability amount and what your home could sell for.

Flood Zone and Insurance

Flood zones in Silver Lakes depend on which side of Pines Boulevard the home is on. Of 7 addresses I checked north of Pines Boulevard, all 7 are in FEMA Zone X, outside the high-risk area. Of 7 addresses I checked south of Pines Boulevard, 6 are in Zone AE, a Special Flood Hazard Area where lenders require flood insurance, and 1 is in Zone X. MLS listings from the last two years show the same pattern. Even in Zone X, flood insurance is usually inexpensive and worth considering. If the home is in Zone AE, ask for the elevation certificate early. Pembroke Pines is a Class 5 community in FEMA's Community Rating System, which can lower National Flood Insurance Program premiums by up to 25%. Most homes are from the early 1990s, so the age of the roof, impact windows or shutters, and the wind mitigation report make the biggest difference in homeowners insurance.

Getting Around

Pines Boulevard runs through the middle of Silver Lakes. From the middle of the community it's about 4 miles to The Shops at Pembroke Gardens and I-75, about 5 miles to Pembroke Lakes Mall, about 7 miles to Pembroke Pines City Hall and about 18 miles to Fort Lauderdale Hollywood International Airport. These are driving distances, and times vary with traffic.

Silver Lakes FAQ

Where is Silver Lakes in Pembroke Pines?

In the far west of the city, on both sides of Pines Boulevard, roughly between NW 21st Street and Pembroke Road, ZIP code 33029. The SilverLakes community continues south of Pembroke Road into Miramar.

Is Silver Lakes a gated community?

Some of its neighborhoods are gated and others are not. Gates are maintained by each neighborhood's sub-association, and a few are manned. A security patrol covers the whole community. Check the specific neighborhood.

How much are the HOA fees in Silver Lakes?

There is a quarterly master association fee, which includes bulk cable TV and internet, plus, for most homes, a separate neighborhood sub-association fee. In MLS listings sold over the last two years, the association fee shown was usually about $190 to $310 a month, and the sub-association fee can be extra. Ask for both before you buy.

Can you rent out a home in Silver Lakes?

Only with restrictions: a 12-month minimum lease, at least a year of ownership first, a 10% rental cap per neighborhood and tenant approval, and some neighborhoods allow no rentals at all.

What do homes in Silver Lakes sell for?

From November 2024 to November 2026, recorded sales on the Pembroke Pines side had a median of about $703,000, with most between about $580,000 and $950,000.

Is Silver Lakes in a flood zone?

It depends on the side. The addresses I checked north of Pines Boulevard were in Zone X, and most of those south of Pines Boulevard were in Zone AE, where lenders require flood insurance. Check each address.

How does Amendment 3 affect Silver Lakes property taxes?

For Florida residents with homestead, the non-school exemption rises to $150,000 in 2027 and $250,000 in 2028. Current homestead owners don't need to reapply. People who move to Florida after December 31, 2026 start at $50,000 and get the larger amount in their fifth year of homestead.
