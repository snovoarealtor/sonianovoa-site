Pembroke Falls at a Glance

Homes: about 2,066 single-family homes in seven plats (Phases 1, 2, 4, 4A, 5, 6 and 7). The Pembroke Falls plats also include about 620 townhomes; this page focuses on the single-family homes.
Built: 1996 to 2004, most around 1999.
Sizes: mostly 4 bedrooms, with 3 and 5 bedroom models too. Most are about 2,150 to 3,100 square feet (median about 2,580), and the largest are over 6,000.
Lots: most are about 6,500 to 12,400 square feet (median about 9,000).
Enclaves: the community is made up of smaller neighborhoods, such as Bimini Bay, Serenity Bay, Laguna Pointe, Cascades and Crystal Harbor.
Water: lakes run through the community, and about a third of recent sales were waterfront.
Gated: yes, with four guard-gated entrances, 24-hour security and an 18,000 square foot clubhouse at 1651 NW 136th Avenue.
For sale now: 11 homes listed in November 2026, which is only about 2 months of supply.
Streets: roughly NW 10th to NW 24th Streets between NW 128th and NW 145th Avenues, ZIP code 33028.
Public schools assigned: Lakeside Elementary, Walter C. Young Middle and Charles W. Flanagan High. Check with Broward County Public Schools for the current assignments.

The HOA and Amenities

Pembroke Falls has one mandatory homeowners association for the whole community, managed by KW Property Management. In 2025 and 2026 MLS listings, HOA dues were about $360 to $375 a month, and they typically include cable TV, high-speed internet and 24-hour security, with some listings also noting alarm monitoring. Ask for the exact amount, what it includes and any special assessments before you buy. The 18,000 square foot clubhouse has a fitness center and aerobics studio with classes, activity rooms and a ballroom, plus a heated pool with a spa and a children's wading pool, lighted tennis courts (the HOA lists 8, and some recent listings mention pickleball courts too), basketball courts and a lakeside walking park. Residents can reserve clubhouse facilities and register visitors for the gates online. The community also has stocked lakes, tot lots and miles of sidewalks. Like most gated communities, Pembroke Falls has rules on exterior changes and parking, and recent listings note that boats, RVs, trucks and trailers can't be parked at the home. Most listings show the community as OK to lease, and many note that the association has to approve buyers or tenants, so ask the seller for the current rules and approval process before you sign.

Property Taxes and Amendment 3

Taxes reset after a sale. At a price of about $885,000, plan on roughly $16,300 a year with homestead or about $17,100 without it (2026 Pembroke Pines rates, including the city fire assessment and drainage fee).

Florida voters passed Amendment 3 on November 3, 2026. For Florida residents with homestead, the exemption on non-school taxes goes from about $51,000 to $150,000 in 2027 and $250,000 in 2028, which at 2026 Pembroke Pines rates is about $1,200 less a year in 2027 and about $2,450 less a year in 2028. For a homesteaded Pembroke Falls home taxed at about $885,000, that would bring the bill from about $16,300 to roughly $15,100 in 2027 and $13,900 in 2028, if tax rates stay the same. If you already have homestead, you don't need to reapply. If you move to Florida after December 31, 2026, the non-school exemption starts at $50,000 and the larger amount begins in your fifth year of homestead. School taxes and the Save Our Homes cap don't change. See my Amendment 3 page, and ask me for an estimate on a specific home.

Selling in Pembroke Falls: Save Our Homes and Portability

About 85% of Pembroke Falls homes have a homestead exemption, and on the 2026 county roll the typical homesteaded home here is assessed about $360,000 below its market value because of the Save Our Homes cap. When you sell, the buyer's taxes reset to market value, but you can usually transfer up to $500,000 of that Save Our Homes benefit to your next Florida homestead if you set it up within about three years. That's called portability, and it can make moving up or downsizing within Florida much more affordable than people expect. I'm happy to estimate your portability amount and what your home could sell for.

Flood Zone and Insurance

Most of Pembroke Falls is in a FEMA flood zone. Of 13 addresses I checked across the neighborhood, 10 are in Zone AE (base flood elevation 6 feet), a Special Flood Hazard Area where lenders require flood insurance, and 3 are in Zone X. Because the homes were built in the late 1990s and 2000s to newer elevation standards, an elevation certificate often helps lower the flood premium, so ask for it early. Pembroke Pines is a Class 5 community in FEMA's Community Rating System, which can lower National Flood Insurance Program premiums by up to 25%. For homeowners insurance, the age of the roof, impact windows or shutters, and the wind mitigation report make the biggest difference.

Getting Around

From the middle of Pembroke Falls it's under a mile to the clubhouse, about 2 miles to Memorial Hospital West, under 3 miles to Pembroke Lakes Mall, about 4.5 miles to Pembroke Pines City Hall and about 14 miles to Fort Lauderdale Hollywood International Airport. I-75 runs along the west side of the community. These are driving distances, and times vary with traffic.
